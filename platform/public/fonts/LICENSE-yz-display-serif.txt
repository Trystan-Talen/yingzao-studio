yz-display-serif.woff
=====================

Subset of Noto Serif SC (思源宋体), weight 900, containing only the glyphs used in
映造 Studio display headlines. Renamed "YZ Display Serif" to avoid clashing with an
installed copy of the full family. Noto Serif SC has no Reserved Font Name.

Source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/notoserifsc

Full text: https://openfontlicense.org/open-font-license-official-text/

To add characters (e.g. a new headline), re-subset the full Noto Serif SC [wght]
file at wght=900 with fontTools (pyftsubset) and replace this file.
