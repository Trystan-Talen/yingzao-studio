yz-display-brand.woff
=====================

Subset of ZCOOL QingKe HuangYou (站酷庆科黄油体), containing only the glyphs used in
映造 Studio display headlines. Renamed "YZ Display Brand" to avoid clashing with an
installed copy of the full family.

Source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/zcoolqingkehuangyou

(https://www.github.com/googlefonts/zcool-qingke-huangyou)

This Font Software is licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1.
Full text: https://openfontlicense.org/open-font-license-official-text/

To add characters (e.g. a new headline), re-subset the full
ZCOOLQingKeHuangYou-Regular.ttf with fontTools (pyftsubset) and replace this file.

yz-display-serif.woff (Noto Serif SC Heavy subset) is kept as the previously
evaluated alternative; it is not referenced by the stylesheets.
